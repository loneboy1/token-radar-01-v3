Launch Scope v3

A token launch radar. Every pre-token launch candidate ranked by its expected launch value — a fitted token value times the odds a token ever lists, both computed from the snapshot's own figures. No protocol is named by hand; the board is whatever the figures say.

How to read this board

  1. Value, fitted — not picked. log(market cap) is fitted by least squares on the protocols whose token already trades. The coefficients come from the snapshot, and the fit is tested held-out two ways (leave-one-out and walk-forward). The numbers are on the strip above.
  2. Odds from peers. A protocol's odds of ever listing are the rate at which protocols in its own age-and-size cohort already record a token.
  3. Verdict first. Every row opens with FARM, THIN or SKIP — the $/hour of farming it at $10,000, after gas and the forgone yield. Detail first, model second.
  4. Nothing is hidden. All 758 protocols in the snapshot are accounted for below: ranked, live at market value, token-recorded with no price, or excluded with a reason.
